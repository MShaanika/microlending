What's inside

Eight systems most lenders run separately. Here, they're one.

End-to-end loan lifecycle

Public and agent-assisted applications, scoring, approval, disbursement, and repayment scheduling — one record from first click to final installment.

Collections that run themselves

Debit orders placed, tracked, rescheduled and cancelled directly on Collexia's EnDO network — no manual bank runs, no chasing a spreadsheet.

Credit bureau, at the point of decision

Live Creditinfo Namibia checks during assessment, plus a full dual-control workflow for listing and removing public defaults — every step logged.

Full accounting, not a side ledger

Double-entry general ledger, bank reconciliation and fixed assets, with Balance Sheet, P&L, Cash Flow and Tax Computation generated live from real transactions — not rebuilt in a spreadsheet every quarter.

NAMFISA, not an afterthought

Levy and duty stamp handling built into every disbursement, with retention and reporting rules that match the Act — not bolted on later.

Governance you can prove

Maker-checker approval on every sensitive action — write-offs, bureau submissions, and more — with a complete, unbroken audit trail behind each one.

Bank statements read for you

Upload a statement and the system parses income and spending, then calculates Debt-to-Income on the spot — a consistent, defensible affordability read on every application.

HR and payroll, not a side system

Recruitment, leave, promotions, staff loans and payroll — run the people side of the business next to the loan book, not in a separate package.
